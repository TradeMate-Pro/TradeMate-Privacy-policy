100% Client-Side Computation & Confidentiality

TradeMate executes all stock averaging computations, options lot pricing, multi-tranche exit strategies, trade risk simulations, and SIP compounding models exclusively on your local device. We do not operate remote user databases to collect, store, or monitor your financial calculations.

01 Information We Do NOT Collect

Your financial independence and privacy are fundamental. When using TradeMate:

  • No Broker Credentials: We never request broker logins, trading passwords, Demat IDs, or API keys.
  • No Personal Financial Records: We do not log or store stock symbols, buy quantities, prices, or trade journals on any server.
  • No Personal Identification (PII): No names, email registrations, phone numbers, or PAN documentation are required.

02 Local Device Storage (localStorage)

The application utilizes sandboxed browser and device storage (localStorage) strictly for persistent offline functionality:

Currency Symbol Remembers your selected currency (₹ INR, $ USD, € EUR, £ GBP).
Saved Calculation History Stores snapshots you explicitly choose to save to History.
Pro Pass & Entitlements Caches 24-Hour Pro Pass expiry tokens earned via Rewarded Ads.

Note: You can permanently wipe all locally saved calculation history anytime inside the Settings → Clear History menu.

03 Advertising & Google AdMob

The free tier of TradeMate is supported by advertisements served through Google AdMob (Banner, Rewarded 24-Hour Pass, and Interstitial Video ads).

Google AdMob may process pseudonymous device identifiers (e.g., Google Advertising ID) in accordance with Google's Privacy & Terms. You can reset or opt out of personalized advertising in your Android system settings, or upgrade to TradeMate Pro for a 100% ad-free experience.

04 Device Permissions (Export & Share)

TradeMate accesses device features only upon explicit user action:

  • Downloads & Files: Used solely to save generated PDF reports and PNG calculation cards to your device.
  • System Share Sheet: Invokes your operating system's native share dialog when you tap "Share as PDF" or "Share Setup Card".

⚠️ Regulatory & Mathematical Disclaimer

TradeMate is strictly a quantitative calculation and arithmetic utility. We are NOT SEBI-registered, nor licensed financial advisors. We do not provide buy/sell signals, stock recommendations, portfolio management, or financial advice. All figures are algorithmic estimations based on user-entered parameters.
