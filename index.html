Privacy Policy

App: TradeMate - Stock & Options Calculator | Last Updated: August 2026

1. Overview

TradeMate ("we", "our", or "the application") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy outlines how our application operates, what data is processed, and your rights regarding data privacy.

Core Privacy Principle: TradeMate is designed as an offline-first financial calculator utility. We do NOT collect, sell, transmit, or store any personal data, trade records, brokerage credentials, or financial numbers on remote servers. All your calculations remain strictly on your local device.

2. Information Collection and Use

TradeMate does not require user registration or account creation. Specifically:

  • No Personal Identifiable Information (PII): We do not collect your name, phone number, physical address, or email address.
  • No Financial or Trading Credentials: TradeMate does not connect to any brokerage accounts (e.g., Zerodha, Groww, AngelOne, Robinhood) and does not store account logins, balances, or PINs.
  • Local Storage: Any trade logs, calculation presets, or scrapbook notes you save are stored locally on your device storage (via secure LocalStorage/IndexedDB). You can wipe this data at any time by clearing the application data from your device settings or using the in-app Reset options.

3. Third-Party Services & Advertising

The application may display advertisements served by third-party advertising networks (such as Google AdMob) or utilize basic crash diagnostic tools. These services may collect standard anonymous device identifiers to serve non-personalized or personalized ads as permitted by Google Play Developer policies:

  • Google Play Services: Used for app updates, licensing, and security integrity.
  • Google AdMob (if enabled): May use an anonymous Advertising ID to display ads. You can manage or reset your Advertising ID via your Android device's Settings > Google > Ads.

For more information on how Google uses data, please visit Google Privacy & Terms.

4. Device Permissions

TradeMate requires minimal permissions to operate:

  • Internet Access: Required only for serving advertisements and verifying Play Store licensing.
  • Network State: Checks for active internet connectivity to load ad banners.
  • No Dangerous Permissions: TradeMate does NOT request access to your Contacts, Camera, Microphone, SMS, or GPS Location.

5. Children's Privacy

TradeMate is a financial utility intended for general audiences and trading enthusiasts. We do not knowingly collect any information from children under the age of 13. If you believe that a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately.

6. Security of Your Data

Because all calculation data resides locally on your personal device, the security of your stored notes and trade calculations is protected by your device's operating system security (passcodes, biometric locks, and app sandbox security).

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically to reflect app updates or regulatory changes. Any modifications will be posted directly to this publicly accessible URL.
