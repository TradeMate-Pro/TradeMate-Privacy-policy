100% Client-Side & Zero Remote Data Storage

TradeMate is engineered to perform all stock averaging, options lot computations, multi-tranche profit booking targets, trade simulations, and SIP compounding algorithms entirely on your local device. We do not operate remote databases to collect, store, or transmit your financial inputs.

01 Information We Do NOT Collect

Your trading and financial confidentiality is fundamental. When using TradeMate:

  • No Broker Credentials: We never ask for broker logins, Demat accounts, API keys, or trading passwords.
  • No Personal Identifiable Information (PII): No names, email addresses, phone numbers, PAN cards, or ID proofs are required to use calculators.
  • No Financial Records: We do not collect or upload your buy prices, quantity sizes, trade notes, or portfolio values to any server.

02 Local Device Storage (localStorage)

The application stores lightweight application state directly inside your browser or device sandboxed storage (localStorage) to enable core offline features:

Currency Settings Remembers your preferred currency symbol (e.g., ₹ INR, $ USD, € EUR).
Saved Calculation History Locally caches snapshots you explicitly choose to save via the "Save results" button.
Pro Pass & Entitlements Stores 24-Hour Pro Pass expiry tokens earned via Rewarded Ads and in-app purchase receipts.

Note: You can permanently clear all stored local data at any time from inside the Settings → Clear History menu.

03 Third-Party Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free tier of TradeMate is supported by non-intrusive advertisements served through Google AdMob (Banner, Rewarded 24-Hour Pro Pass, and Interstitial Video ads).

Google AdMob may collect and process pseudonymous identifiers (such as Google Advertising ID / IDFA) and general device telemetry to deliver relevant advertisements in compliance with Google's Privacy & Terms.

  • You can reset or opt out of personalized ad tracking via your Android device Settings → Google → Ads.
  • Subscribing to TradeMate Pro or activating an ad-earned 24-Hour Pro Pass completely disables all banner and interstitial ads across every screen.

04 Device Permissions (PDF Export & Image Sharing)

TradeMate includes client-side utilities to download PDF reports and export PNG setup cards:

  • Storage / Files: Used solely when you trigger "Download PDF" or "Download Image" to save the rendered calculation report to your device's Downloads directory.
  • System Share Dialog: Invokes the native OS share sheet only when you tap "Share as PDF" or "Share Setup Card". No data is shared without your explicit action.

⚠️ SEBI & Financial Calculation Disclaimer

TradeMate is strictly a mathematical arithmetic utility and quantitative data visualizer.

TradeMate is NOT registered with SEBI (Securities and Exchange Board of India), FINRA, SEC, or any other financial regulatory authority. We do not provide buy/sell signals, stock recommendations, portfolio management, or financial advisory services. All outputs are algorithmic estimates based on your entered parameters. Trading in equities, derivatives (options & futures), and financial securities involves significant market risk.
