100% Client-Side & Offline Privacy

TradeMate processes all stock averaging, options lot computations, multi-tranche profit booking exits, trade risk projections, and compounding tables entirely on your local device. We do not operate remote databases or store your financial figures on our servers.

01 Information We Do NOT Collect

We respect your confidential trading records. When using TradeMate:

  • No Broker Logins: We never request broker credentials, Demat account keys, or API tokens.
  • No Personal Financial Records: We do not log or transmit your stock names, quantities, buy prices, or trade logs.
  • No PII Required: No names, email addresses, phone numbers, or PAN numbers are collected to use calculation tools.

02 Local Device Storage (localStorage)

The application saves lightweight data directly inside your browser or device sandboxed storage (localStorage) to power local functionality:

Currency Symbol Remembers your preferred currency (e.g., ₹ INR, $ USD, € EUR, £ GBP).
Saved History Locally stores snapshots you explicitly choose to save via the "Save results" button.
Pro Pass Tokens Stores 24-Hour Pro Pass expiry tokens earned via Rewarded Ads.

Tip: You can clear all saved history anytime inside the Settings → Clear History section.

03 Third-Party Advertising (Google AdMob)

Free versions of TradeMate may display advertisements served via Google AdMob (Banner, Rewarded 24-Hour Pass, and Interstitial Video ads) to fund continuous maintenance.

Google AdMob may process pseudonymous identifiers (e.g., Google Advertising ID) and device technical telemetry subject to Google's Privacy & Terms. You can reset or opt out of personalized ad tracking in your Android device settings or upgrade to TradeMate Pro for an ad-free experience.

04 Device Permissions (PDF & Image Export)

TradeMate requests device permissions strictly for user-requested tasks:

  • Storage / Downloads: Used only when you trigger "Download PDF" or "Download Image" to save the rendered calculation file directly to your device.
  • System Share: Opens your device’s native share dialog when you tap "Share as PDF" or "Share Setup Card".

⚠️ Regulatory & Financial Disclaimer

TradeMate is strictly a quantitative calculation and arithmetic utility. We are NOT SEBI-registered, nor licensed financial advisors. We do not provide buy/sell signals, tips, portfolio management, or investment recommendations. All outputs are mathematical estimations based on your inputs.
