Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 27, 2026

Privacy First: TradeMate is designed to work completely offline on your device. We do not collect, store, transmit, or share your financial figures, trade inputs, notes, or stock records.

1. Information Collection and Use

TradeMate does not require you to create an account, log in with broker credentials, or provide personal identification details (such as names, phone numbers, or PAN numbers).

  • Calculation Inputs: All numbers, buy prices, lot quantities, target percentages, and trade notes entered into calculators are processed exclusively on your device's memory.
  • Local Storage: Your preferred currency setting, saved calculation snapshots, and 24-Hour Pro Pass expiry tokens are stored locally on your device using standard sandboxed storage.

2. Third-Party Advertising & Services

The free version of TradeMate may display advertisements served via Google AdMob (Banner, Rewarded, or Interstitial Ads) to support development.

  • Google AdMob may collect pseudonymous advertising identifiers subject to Google's Privacy Policy.
  • You can opt out of personalized ad tracking in your device's Android settings or upgrade to TradeMate Pro for an ad-free experience.

3. Export & Device Permissions

TradeMate requests device permissions strictly for user-initiated actions:

  • Storage / Files: Used only when you tap "Download PDF" or "Download Image" to save reports to your local Downloads directory.
  • System Share: Invokes your operating system's standard share dialog when you explicitly tap "Share as PDF" or "Share Setup Card".

4. Financial Disclaimer

TradeMate is purely a mathematical calculation utility. We are not SEBI-registered or licensed financial advisors. The app does not offer investment tips, stock recommendations, or portfolio management services.
